✦ FAQ

Questions? Answered.

Still curious? Our team replies in under 2 hours, 7 days a week.

Is Bliss actually free?

Yes — genuinely. Every command, including AI chat and AutoMod, is free forever with a 1,000 message monthly limit. Pro unlocks unlimited messages and image generation. No credit card is required to start.

How long does setup take?

About 30 seconds. Click “Add to Discord”, pick your server, authorize, and Bliss is live. AutoMod and welcome messages configure themselves based on your server's existing vibe. No dashboards, no config files.

Does Bliss read all my messages?

Only messages in channels where it's active, and only to respond or moderate. We never train models on your data, never sell it, and you can wipe your server's memory at any time with /privacy purge.

What happens if Bliss goes down?

We run on multi-region infrastructure with automatic failover and maintain 99.99% uptime. If we ever drop below that in a billing month, Pro and Ultra subscribers get an automatic credit — no support ticket needed.

Can I customize how Bliss talks?

Absolutely. Describe the personality in plain English — “wholesome camp counselor”, “sarcastic older sibling”, “feral goblin with a heart of gold” — and Bliss adapts instantly. You can also set per-channel tones.

Is Bliss affiliated with Discord?

No. Bliss is an independent third-party app built on the official Discord API and fully compliant with Discord's developer terms. We're just huge fans.
